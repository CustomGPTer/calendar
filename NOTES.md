Calendar 1.0.1

- Room to scroll past the Add bar: every page now leaves space at the bottom for the Add bar and your phone's navigation buttons, so the last item can always be scrolled into full view (month, day, week, year, schedule and the event editor).
- The month and the day's list under it now scroll together as one page. Pulling down at the top still opens the full-screen month.
- An empty day shows one short line and one sticker button, beside the date.
- Week and day views: when a day has more all-day items than fit, the "+1" now sits under that day.

Installs over 1.0.0 and keeps everything.

Calendar.apk is 24.7 MB (25,937,397 bytes). Android 8 or later.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/calendar/
