First release of Calendar: a private calendar for Android that never goes online.

- Year (with a busy-day heat map), month (compact and expanded), week, three-day, day and schedule views, with zoom animations between them.
- Events and reminders, every kind of repeat, full-screen reminders with Snooze and Done, drag to move and resize, undo, quick add in plain English and search.
- Bank holidays for England and Wales (Scotland, Northern Ireland, Ireland and nine more countries in Settings), worked out on the phone.
- Birthdays and anniversaries with ages, countdowns, templates, checklists, encrypted attachments, stickers and photo stickers cut out on the phone.
- Optional shift patterns with hours worked, and optional call-back reminders after missed calls.
- Copy from the phone's Google, Samsung or Outlook calendars, with a note on who can see each one; .ics import and export; share an event by QR code, file or text.
- Encrypted daily backups to a folder you choose, and restore on any phone with your password.
- PIN and fingerprint app lock, privacy screen, five widgets and five themes.

Calendar.apk is 24.7 MB (25,935,373 bytes). Android 8 or later.
