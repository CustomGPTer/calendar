What Calendar does not do

Calendar has no syncing between devices, no shared calendars, no web or desktop version, no weather and no video-call links, because it never goes online. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Google Calendar syncs across phones, watches, computers and the web, shares calendars with other people and adds events from Gmail. Samsung Calendar shows the weather and can show other calendars on the phone, including Google's. Both update themselves from their app stores.
